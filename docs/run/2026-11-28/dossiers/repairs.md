### 3.2 Boiler flue extension (repairs.02)

- Gas Safe Register's guidance: a plume management kit must not be used to get round the requirement to place the flue terminal correctly, unless the boiler maker's instructions allow it; kits are installed strictly to the maker's instructions, and an engineer can seek a concession through the register where needed [4][5]. The legal basis cited is the Gas Safety (Installation and Use) Regulations 1998, regulation 27(5): no person shall install a flue other than in a safe position [4].
- Retail plume kits (for example Baxi 60 mm with a 1 m extension, Vaillant 100 mm) carry the line that gas boilers and accessories must be installed by a Gas Safe registered engineer [6][7]. Kits are model-specific, which is why the ticket records make, model and flue diameter first.
- Whether DIY fitting of a plume kit is specifically a criminal offence was not confirmed (UNVERIFIED); the ticket simply says it is gas work for a Gas Safe engineer.
- The Gas Safe Register site refused an automated fetch (UNVERIFIED that the engineer search is at the home page path; it is linked from there) [8].

### 3.3 Render patching (repairs.03)

- Tap to find hollow render and remove it back to sound edges; clean, dust-free masonry; bond with SBR outside (PVA re-wets in rain); a 4 to 1 plastering sand to cement mix; two coats if deeper than about 20 mm; finish with a wooden float; cure 3 to 4 weeks before painting because fresh cement is highly alkaline [9][10][11]. Traditional lime render must never be patched with cement [9]. The ticket checks the render type first.

### 3.4 Derelict property next door (repairs.04)

Doncaster Council's routes as published on 2026-11-28 (no personal data used in the search):
- **Nuisance Team** (Housing, empty properties): reports of an empty property that is insecure, has rubbish accumulating, or affects the area, by email from the council's page; enforcement is a last resort after advice [12].
- **Building Control, dangerous structures**: a 24-hour, 365-day emergency service; the page says to telephone rather than email for danger; officers assess the risk and can serve a Building Act 1984 section 77 notice or act under section 78 for imminent danger [13].
- **Public health team, unsatisfactory condition or appearance of buildings and land**: section 215 of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990 notices for land or buildings harming the area's amenity [14].
- No council page mentions exposed electrical sockets. If they are reachable from a public place or his garden and could be live, that is a danger question for Building Control (UNVERIFIED whether they treat it as a dangerous structure; the ticket phones them first if in doubt).

### 3.5 Air-conditioning rattle (repairs.05)

- Loose cabinet panel screws are the most common cause of outdoor unit rattles, then worn or missing anti-vibration pads, an unlevel unit, debris or a fan problem; power off before opening panels; some vibration in operation is normal (Fujitsu, LG and HVAC guides) [15][16]. Matches Phil's "check the right-side panel fixings before wedging".

### 3.6 Fly screens (repairs.06)

- Off-the-shelf magnetic door screens were about £14.50 to £43 at UK marketplaces, fitted with adhesive or pins without drilling; made-to-measure magnetic screens from about £29.99 per window or door; roller fly screens are made to measure, screw-fixed and fitted by a capable DIYer in under an hour by the seller's account [17][18][19]. Vendor figures, single listings.

### 3.7 Cast iron weights (repairs.07)

- Brush loose rust off; a 50/50 vinegar and water soak for 24 to 72 hours for heavy rust; scrub, rinse, dry straight away; a light oil coat; store dry [20]. Steel brushes scratch cast iron; brass or nylon are softer [21]. Hobbyist sources.
