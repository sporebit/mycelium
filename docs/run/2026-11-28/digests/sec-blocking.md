## 1. First answers

### Section 9: Blocking (SEC; rides on the network DNS filter from topic 2) (brief lines 279-308)

1. The outlet list beyond BBC, Daily Mail and ITV: **a research ticket proposes outlets with a reason each; he approves each one**.
2. Why: **he does not trust what they report, they affect his mood, he does not want to give them attention or money**.
3. Instead of news: **a weekly digest from sources he chooses**.
4. News strength: **block, with an "open anyway" page** (changed from the earlier soft hide).
5. Reasons the research may use, all four: **upheld regulator rulings and published corrections, independent fact-checker records, ownership and funding, examples he supplies**.
6. Adult-site strength: **block, with an "open anyway" page**.
7. Adult-site block is for: **him, guests on the guest network, and ready for a child later** (not "the whole household").
8. Off switch: **him, at any time**.
9. His devices in scope: **iPhone, Pixel, main PC, TVs and streaming sticks**.
10. Kirsty's devices: **unfiltered for news**; per-device filtering; she can opt in.
11. Guest network: **adult sites and security blocklists, not news**.
12. Away from home: **the same filter everywhere** via a profile on each phone.
13. Bypasses closed on his devices: **browsers' own encrypted DNS and iCloud Private Relay; VPN and proxy apps**.
14. Inside X, Reddit and similar apps: **leave the apps alone**.
15. YouTube: **channel blocking on the PC; "don't recommend" on phones and TVs**.
16. Search: **a results-filter extension on the PC; phones rely on the block page**.
17. Shared TVs (LG, Samsung, Fire Stick): **ask Kirsty first, a ticket**; technical tickets wait on it.
18. Sky Glass: **research what can be hidden** before any change.
19. TV licence: **a research ticket on the current rules** for his set-up, from the official source.
20. Built-in news feeds (Discover, Apple News, Windows widgets, start pages): **turned off entirely**, a ticket per device.
21. Emails and app notifications from listed outlets: **cleared in the email migration's sender sweep**; apps removed.
22. Alexa: **change the default news and radio providers, disable listed skills**.
23. Podcasts and radio from listed outlets: **unfollow and remove the apps**.
24. Record: **a weekly count in the Sunday close-out** (hits per list, how often opened anyway).
25. That record is **owner-only**, never on the wall, never shared.
26. Also carried by the filter: **scam, malware and tracker lists; time limits on social apps**.
27. The digest is read **in the Sunday close-out, beside Finds**.
28. **Per-person profiles from the start** (him, Kirsty, guests, a future child).
29. Proof: **a test run from every device and profile**, re-run in the monthly review.
30. Done means: **30 days without seeing a listed outlet unless he chose to**.

## 2. Round 2 extras

### Section 9b: Blocking, extras (brief lines 518-527)

- **Block page shows:** his next thing from Mycelium with a Start button, how many times he has landed there this week, and one of his saved quotes.
- **"Open anyway":** different per list, quick for news; **a 10-minute wait for adult sites**.
- **A week with many open-anyways:** the Boys raise it bluntly in the Sunday close-out and ask why, **and** the wait gets longer the following week.
- **BBC / ITV entertainment, sport, iPlayer:** everything from a listed outlet is blocked, not only news.
- **Need-to-know:** official alerts only (weather warnings, government emergency alerts), pushed through Mycelium.
- **Coming off the list:** a yearly review against the same criteria.
- **Digest:** as many items as there are, grouped by topic.
- **Digest topics, all four:** technology and AI; the health service and his own field; Doncaster and South Yorkshire; national and world affairs.
- **Social app limits:** a daily minute allowance per app **and** blocked during set hours.
- **Weak spots, all four** (rules tightest then): in bed in the morning, breaks during work, evenings on the sofa, late at night.
