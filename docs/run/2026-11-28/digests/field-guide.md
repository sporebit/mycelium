## 2. Round 2 extras

### Section 12b: Guide template, extras (brief lines 556-565)

- **The format is called a Field guide.**
- **Signature motion:** the progress bar is a thread that grows and branches as steps are ticked; each phase is a branch; a finished phase fruits.
- **Sound:** a soft tick per step, a fuller chime when a phase completes (off by default on the standalone page; follows Mycelium's sound setting in the app).
- **Real time:** time between ticks is recorded and estimates correct themselves.
- **The hard part is named up front** in the top box ("the tricky bit is step 7, and here is why").
- **Interrupted:** a Park button, one line on where he stopped, back on tomorrow's list.
- **By voice** (steps read aloud; "done", "next", "repeat"): yes, once the glasses route exists.
- **Type:** a warm, readable face; monospace only for values, commands and numbers.
- **Flight plan view:** every sitting, its time, what it needs and the hard part, on one screen, before starting.
- **Quality gate:** Claude has run every step it can against the real thing or a sandbox, and says which it could not; untested steps are marked.
