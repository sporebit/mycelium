Kirsty's tickets (K) count like Claude's for Phil's time. Five tickets the red team called visibly under-pointed (home.14, rescue.03, rescue.04, net.07, mail.24) are counted at double. mail.24's batches are priced as "High rows divided by 10" sittings once mail.04 reports the count; until then it carries its 8 points doubled.
